02 / RESEARCH

Researching for safer AI.

My current research explores the security and reliability of Retrieval-Augmented Generation systems.

CURRENT RESEARCH PROJECT In progress

CTG-RAG: A Cascaded, Calibrated and Explainable Trust Guard for Defending Retrieval-Augmented Generation Against Multiple Poisoning and Injection Attacks

The project investigates how malicious or misleading retrieved content can influence RAG systems. It proposes a trust-aware security layer between retrieval and generation, assessing retrieved passages before they reach the language model.

01

Trust assessment

Evaluate retrieved passages using multiple trust-related signals.

02

Explainable filtering

Identify suspicious content and provide reasons for trust decisions.

03

Uncertainty awareness

Handle uncertain retrieval results rather than blindly passing them forward.
